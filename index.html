HOW IT WORKS

The 6-stage discharge state machine

MGH → PF → RTS → LOA → Settlement → Cleared. Every transition is timestamped, auditable, and visible to billing, liaison, and family at once. The HMO Final eLOA alone causes 80%+ of today's delay bottlenecks — while PHAPi reports ₱4–10B locked in RTH and disputed claims.

  1. MGH trigger — Doctor signs "May Go Home." Countdown starts (T0).
  2. PF sign-off — Attending confirms fee on mobile; excess PF flagged instantly. today: 1–3 hrs → seconds
  3. RTS clearance — Pharmacy return-to-stock audited; phantom charges removed. today: 1–2 hrs → minutes
  4. Final eLOA — Pre-cleansed payload to HMO; approval in seconds, not hours. today: 2–4 hrs → seconds
  5. Settlement — Patient pays or e-signs RA 9439 note on their phone. today: 0.5–1 hr queue → 1 tap
  6. Cleared + QR pass — Lobby verifies pass + 4-digit PIN. Bed turns over.
